# Bundled font notices

This application bundles five additional open-source Chinese font files:

- Noto Sans SC Variable
- Noto Serif SC Variable
- Source Han Sans CN Regular
- Source Han Sans CN Light
- Source Han Sans CN Bold

Noto and Source Han fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1. The fonts are bundled without modification. The full license text
is included in `OFL-1.1.txt` in this directory.

Upstream projects:

- https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
- https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans
